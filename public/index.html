Help, Regulations & Live Feedback Center

Learn how Campfire works across Anime, Gaming & Entertainment hubs, read community guidelines, and chat live to suggest new titles, categories, or features!

📖 FAQ & System Guides 🛡️ Community Regulations 🔧 Support & Troubleshooting 💬 Live Suggestions & Feedback Chat

📖 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & System Guides

🤖 Real-Time AI Recommendations & Matching

Our recommendation engine updates in 100% real-time. Every time you track titles across hubs, update your watch/play progress, rate items (1-5 stars), or change status (Watching/Playing, Completed, Dropped), your affinity matrix instantly recalculates match percentages (88%-99%).

🎴 Hourly Curated Swipe Deck

Every hour, Campfire curates 10 fresh recommendations across your selected hub and interests. Swipe right (or click ❤️) to add directly to your Plan list, or swipe left (✕) to pass. You can also undo passes or reset the deck anytime!

🔍 Auto-Importing Missing Titles

Can't find a specific anime, game, or title in the database? Simply type the full title into the Discover search bar. Campfire automatically queries external APIs in real-time, imports the missing title, and saves it permanently to the community database.

🌐 Multi-Hub & Interest Switcher

Seamlessly switch between Anime, Video Games, and upcoming media hubs via the top navbar switcher. Each hub comes with dedicated libraries, status tracking, personalized recommendations, and real-time chat rooms!

🖼️ Avatar Customization & Cropping

Click your avatar in the navbar → "⚙️ Profile Settings". You can choose from 12 Robotic Bottts avatars or 10 stylized character avatars, or click the pencil icon to upload a custom picture and use the built-in circular zoom/pan/rotation cropper.

📊 Dashboard & Activity Analytics

Click "📊 Dashboard" from your profile dropdown. Track your current activity streak (with daily encouragement), all-time streak records, total time spent, completion rate breakdown, and preferred genre & category distributions!

📚 Library & Progress Tracking

Track watched episodes or played hours with smooth debounced stepper controls (+ / -), organize titles into In-Progress, Completed, Plan to Experience, and Dropped, and give personal star ratings to shape your AI recommendations.

👥 Friends & Community Chat

Head to the "👥 Friends" tab to discover enthusiasts, send friend requests, and see who's online. Join the "💬 Community" tab to chat in real-time rooms (#general, #spoilers, #recommendations) or discuss titles directly on their dedicated detail pages!

🛡️ Community Regulations & Moderation Policy

1. Respectful Communication

Treat all community members with kindness. Personal attacks, harassment, trolling, and hate speech are strictly prohibited across all chat rooms and feedback.

2. Automated Bad Word Deletion

All chat and suggestions messages pass through automated profanity filtering. Any message containing vulgar or abusive words is deleted immediately before being broadcast.

3. 2-Warning Account Blocking

Posting bad words or rubbish text triggers an official warning (Warning 1/2). Receiving a 2nd warning automatically bans and blocks the user account permanently.

4. Spoiler Protection

Please post major anime plot twists, manga spoilers, or game endings only in the designated ⚠️ #spoilers chat channel to keep discussions enjoyable for everyone.

🔧 Support & Troubleshooting

🖼️ Media Posters & Cover Art: All posters, game cover art, and banners are served in HD via ultra-fast CDNs. If an image fails to load due to temporary network interruptions, hit refresh or report the title via the live chat box below.
🔑 Account & Login Issues: Campfire uses secure token-based authentication. If you ever get logged out or wish to test demo accounts, click quick demo logins (`sakura@campfire.app`) or sign in with your credentials.
⚡ Performance & Data Sync: High-performance network compression and client-side debouncing ensure immediate sync across all hubs and devices without page reloads.
